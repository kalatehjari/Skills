PE Narrator

Multi-voice audiobook narration with Kokoro, running entirely in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

1 Load the voices

Not loaded
The first load downloads the Kokoro model (about 90–330 MB) and keeps it in the browser's cache, so later loads are quick. Use Chrome or Safari on your Mac.

2 Open a narration script

or paste it below, then No script
Format: SPEAKER: text per line; [PAUSE 1.5s], [SCENE BREAK — PAUSE 2.5s], [SFX: soft interface tone], [SFX: digital interference, 1.5s]. Notes in [brackets] inside a line are not spoken ("slow", "quiet" and "soft" change the delivery). Anything above a line of ===== is ignored.

4 Record

Recorded lines are kept while this page is open, so Stop and Record all carry on where they stopped. ▶ plays a line, ↻ records a new take, ✎ edits the words (then ↻). Listen for any garbled word and give it a new take.
